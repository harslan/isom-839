The Efficient Frontier Explorer

Three assets — stocks, bonds, gold. Every possible mix of them is a dot in risk-return space; the gold curve is the efficient frontier, the set of mixes no rational investor should ever leave. Drag your weights and try to reach it. Then press Crisis and watch diversification — the only free lunch in finance — get eaten.
