Deep Learning Studio: One Recipe, Five Senses

Build five neural networks in Google Colab: one for yes/no, one for numbers, one for pictures, one for music, one for video. No setup, no typing: press Shift + Enter and watch each one learn. Every notebook follows the same five steps and ends with a business decision.

1 · NUMBERSTurn the data into numbers
2 · SHAPEPick a network that fits the data
3 · TRAINGuess, measure, nudge, repeat
4 · TESTGrade it on unseen data
5 · DECIDETurn the prediction into a decision
